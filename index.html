5-stage CP activation system

1. Connect

Call within 24 hours. Introduce yourself, check interest and ask for a short meeting.

2. Understand

Learn locations, client budget, end-user/investor mix, active requirements and current business.

3. Activate

Explain location, product, pricing, payment plan, USPs, inventory and commission process.

4. Generate first lead

Ask for a specific client requirement. Example: “Do you have anyone looking for a 4 BHK in the ₹6–8 Cr range?”

5. Convert

Arrange presentation, site visit, objection handling, negotiation and timely CP updates.

30-day follow-up rhythm

Use judgement: if a CP asks for a different follow-up date, respect that commitment. Avoid repetitive “any leads?” messages; share a useful reason to reconnect.

Qualification questions

  1. Which locations do you primarily operate in?
  2. What is your average client budget?
  3. Do you work with end users, investors or both?
  4. How many active clients are you handling?
  5. What configurations are your clients seeking?
